# Into the Psythara Veil — campaign context

*A self-contained briefing. Paste this whole file into a new chat to bring it up to
speed on the setting, the party and the rules. Assembled 2026-08-20 from
`PLAYER-BACKSTORIES.md`, the design docs in this folder, and the live console.*

**How to read the two kinds of statement below.** Anything under **CANON** is settled
and must not be contradicted or "improved". Anything under **OPEN** is a decision the
GM has not made yet — do not resolve it unasked, and do not invent an answer and then
build on it. If you need one of the open questions settled to answer something, say so
and ask.

---

## The setting in one paragraph

A sci-fi galaxy under corporate dominion, played in D&D 5e-adjacent rules. The players
are **operatives** — contractors, drifters and specialists — carrying a Shoyunuki Corp
field terminal that runs their character sheet. Scattered across the region are shards
of a broken Forerunner machine. The corporations are collecting them for power. Nobody
who is collecting them knows what they are.

---

## CANON — the Obelisk

The spine of the campaign. Everything else hangs off it.

- The Obelisk is a **Forerunner creation**: a machine built to lift living things into
  something else.
- Its makers **split over what it was for**. One faction meant it as uplift for every
  living thing; the other wanted something they could point.
- They **went to war**. During that war, one faction **destroyed their own machine** to
  save the universe.
- It did not die. It **fragmented, and the pieces scattered**. **Nobody knows how many
  shards there are.**
- The entity that speaks to the players through the console is **not a prisoner**. It is
  an **unfinished process, stopped mid-act**, trying to complete ever since.
- **Consent is load-bearing.** The uplift faction built in a safeguard: the machine
  cannot take, it must be given. This is exactly what the other faction objected to. The
  entity has been asking the players for consent since level two.

The console delivers this through a ladder of transmissions tied to character level.
Three load-bearing rungs:

- **L5 — the schism.** *Half of them raised it to lift every living thing / the other
  half wanted something they could point.*
- **L13 — who broke it.** *They broke it themselves to stop me. You are mending it.*
- **L19 — why it must be given.** *The ones who made me required consent. I cannot take
  it.*

Two more that matter for act one:

- **L8** — *It is not an infection. It is proximity. The stone is trying to rewrite what
  stands too close.*
- **L14** — *It took a person. You kept the shape of them.*

---

## CANON — Shoyunuki Corp

- Has **monopolised weaponry, technology and advanced military systems** — including the
  operative console the players carry.
- Is **harvesting shards for the power they produce**, and knows none of the above.
- The resonance sweep on every player's terminal has read **DEGRADED for eleven years**
  and nobody has asked what is on the other side of degraded.
- Divisions seen in play: **Traveller Services** (the species field guide) and
  **Weapon Systems / Armaments & Field Systems** (the weapon catalog).

---

## Act one — Sargasso Station

A customs station. An undead outbreak. The party is **imprisoned aboard** and the
station is in **lockdown**. There is **a shard aboard**.

**CANON:** the "virus" is **shard proximity rewriting living tissue** — overwrite, not
rot. Describe the dead as Forerunner geometry coming through a person, never as decay.
This is already supported mechanically in the console: `radiant` reads *"undead and
Veil-touched creatures take extra"*, `necrotic` reads *"the undead are usually immune,
and some are healed by it instead"*, and the disease ladder runs Ashen Cough −2 → Rust
Fever −4 → Shard Sickness −6 → **Obelisk Plague −8**.

Two catalog items are flagged **required for Sargasso Station progression**: the
**Bio-Scanner** (identifies corpses, scans air for pathogens) and the **Stasis Gloves**
(freezes damaged doors, stabilises reactors). The **Korasi Voidblade** does +50% versus
undead and denies healing.

**OPEN — deliberately unresolved: who has the shard aboard, and why.** Nothing in the
console forecloses it. Do not decide this.

## CANON — how the backstories join the spine

- **Othuun is what came out of the machine.** The first living thing put through it — the
  uplift faction's proof of concept, and the reason the other faction concluded it had to
  be destroyed. *Harbinger of the lightless void* is what "lifting a living thing into
  something else" actually produced. Vorvane's war in heaven and the Forerunner schism
  are **the same war**.
- **Vorvane's 1,000 is wrong.** It is what the corporation in his backstory *catalogued*,
  not the true number. He believes it; finding out otherwise is his beat.
- **Delairah's "seed of existence" is the Obelisk**, seen from the other side — a machine
  built to lift life, scattered in pieces, being collected by corporations. She is
  chasing it under the uplift faction's intention, remembered by someone who never
  learned what it cost. Dramatic irony, not a second mystery.

## OPEN — rulings the GM has not made

1. **Corporate structure.** Three actors exist: **Shoyunuki**, **AstraCalyx Meridian**
   (Sokka's reefs) and Nyra's unnamed *"faceless order"*. Are they three companies, or
   one monopoly with two subsidiaries? Subsidiaries is tidier and gives Nyra's hunt a
   terminus standing in the room — but it is not decided.
2. **Nyra and Sokka share a wound** — both ocean worlds killed by corporate extraction.
   If it is the same event or parent company, Sokka is carrying the answer to Nyra's
   entire arc and does not know it. Not decided.
3. **The two Starseer orders.** Caolan serves the **Rugi**; Quill was raised by the
   **Starseer Guild**. Same institution, two branches, or rivals? If the same, Quill is
   Caolan's future and Caolan is Quill's past. Not decided.
4. **Who has the Sargasso shard, and why.** See above. Not decided.

## The system

- 5e-adjacent. Six ability scores; **AGI is just another name for DEX**.
- **34 classes**, **21 species**. Roles are **Melee / Ranged / Support**, and role sets
  both an AC bonus (+3/+2/+1) and a shield bonus (+4/+3/+2).
- **Shields sit in front of HP.** Damage order is always **Shields → Temp HP → HP**.
  Max shields = 10 + CON modifier + role bonus.
- **Cryo Regeneration** is the long rest; **field cycle** is the short one.
- **Focus** — a small pool: spend a point to add 1d4 after seeing a roll, refresh a spent
  charge, or once per combat refuse to drop to 0 HP.
- Consumables are **stims, med kits, shield cells and chips**, tracked in doses.
- **Progression (designed, not yet built):** level cap 20, an XP-and-skill-tree system,
  **Vox Credits** as currency, and **System Damage** deaths where a knock takes a skill
  node offline. Detailed in `SKILL-TREE-RULEBOOK.md`, `CLASS-BRANCHES.md` and
  `VERTICAL-SLICE.md`.

## The tool

Players use the **Psythara Operative Console** — one self-contained HTML file, ~23 MB,
run offline by double-click. It holds the character sheet, combat tracker, dice, the
weapon catalog, a species field guide, a rules glossary and a manual initiative order.

**Scope rule that matters:** it is a **player-side sheet only**. The DM runs enemy
combat, so enemy-facing effects are *displayed for the DM to apply*, never simulated.
