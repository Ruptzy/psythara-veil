# Player Backstories — Into the Psythara Veil

Source: GM posts in Discord, 8/29/2025 – 2/5/2026. Recorded verbatim in substance;
portraits for all nine are in `Pictures/Players/`.

This file is the canonical roster reference. The **Lore reconciliation** section at
the end lists the places where player-supplied lore and the console's obelisk lore
touch — some of them are opportunities, some need a ruling.

---

## Caolan Shal — the Starseer
*Portrait: `Caohlan.png` · Class: Starseer*

Member of the **Rugi**, an order of psionically gifted beings dedicated to learning
the responsible use of that power. Rugi psionics draw on a connection to the forces
of the universe — the energy of the universe itself and of all living things.

Caolan is a newer member serving under a master Rugi, sent alone on a mission to a
world where he must face a test of his worst fears and weaknesses. He has walked a
fine line on what the order considers acceptable use: misusing his power, and
overreaching into what the Rugi would call *violent usage of power*. He has also,
alongside his master, thwarted oppressive powers moving on innocent peoples.

Outspoken, known to jump the gun. Dedicated to helping those in need by any means
he can muster.

## Echo — the Combat Engineer
*Portrait: `Echo.png` · Player: Sikfrenzy*

Real name buried in service records. Born on a struggling frontier colony, where
survival depended on improvisation. While others trained for glory, Echo trained to
keep people alive — part soldier, part field medic, part mechanic.

During a disastrous drop mission that scattered half his squad and left them presumed
dead, he refused to leave the field until every survivor was accounted for. That
stubbornness earned the callsign **Echo** — the voice that always answered when
others called out in the dark.

> Creed: *no one gets left behind.*

## Vorvane — the Bounty Hunter
*Portrait: `vorvane.png`*

**Ancient being.** ~50,000 years ago there was "a war in heaven" between the largest
interstellar alliances. During that prolonged conflict a weapon was created that
awakened a slumbering void being — a proto-cosmic intelligence named **Othuun**,
given epitaphs such as *harbinger of the lightless void*, *the shrieking god-flesh*,
*Maw of dead suns*.

Othuun became an existential threat, eating entire solar systems. The same weapon
that woke it was improved and used to kill it after 25 years of atrocities. Rather
than dying, it **fragmented its essence into 1,000 shards**.

Those shards existed 49,000 years unperturbed, in drastically different simple
existences:
- some became enormous space whales that phase in and out of existence
- some hop from one simple sentient creature to the next
- some simply exist as amorphous space essence

Nobody noticed the splintering until **1,000 years ago**, when *(mega-corp name TBD)*
heard of research by **Dr. Junias Xerthlot**, whose work on the hard-to-track space
whales led her to realise they might be fragments of Othuun — a story most dismissed
as fairy tale.

The corp, knowing the story true, captured a whale at great cost and began
experimenting toward weaponisation. Over 50 years they took **~300 shards**. They
were careful never to let fragments merge, after one event where **5 merged** into a
violent, powerful creature that escaped containment.

They turned the remaining **295 fragments into symbiotic organisms** meant to control
and infect — but had not finished solving control when the lab was destroyed by a
rebel group funded by competing corporate interests.

## Quill — the Starseer Renegade
*Portrait: `quill.png` · Class: Starseer*

Born in the underbelly of a forgotten city on a lesser-known world. Strange powers
stirred in him from his earliest memories — forces he never asked for and could not
ignore. Parents, neighbours and other children recoiled from the sparks that escaped
when his emotions slipped. To them he was dangerous; to him, simply different.

Cast out, he trained alone for years, fearing a single misstep might harm someone.
The **Starseer Guild** finally came — not with torches or chains but an invitation.
He studied, drilled and meditated, and for a time felt hope. But the more his powers
grew, the more the Guild whispered behind closed doors: what they had called
potential, they began calling danger.

Even among the gifted he was set apart — not celebrated, not trusted, only tolerated.
So he left. He wanders with no banner and no master, seeking not glory or conquest
but **belonging**: people who might see more than a threat, and a life where his
powers could be something greater than a curse.

## Nyra Veylin — the Detective
*Portrait: `Nyra.png`*

Born on an ocean world — a cradle of bioluminescent life and endless currents. Her
people lived in harmony with the tides until corporate ships arrived promising trade
and protection. Then came the tankers: vast reservoirs of toxic waste poured into the
water, poisoning reefs and strangling colonies. In weeks the ocean turned sickly
black; in months, a graveyard.

Her parents, community leaders, resisted — and drowned in that fight. Nyra survived,
but survival was no mercy. Her homeworld was ruined by a faceless order signed in an
office light-years away.

She now works as a detective driven by one obsession: **who gave the order**. She
follows clues through corporate back channels, criminal syndicates and interstellar
underworlds. Every case is another piece; every criminal exposed, a step closer.

**Personality**
- **Relentless** — once locked onto a case she will not let go. Sleep, comfort and
  safety all come second.
- **Hard shell, soft core** — cold and vicious to most, all edge and calculation. In
  truth she still aches for connection, and her compassion leaks out in who she
  chooses to protect: always the powerless, the silenced, the forgotten.
- **Independent** — works alone by choice. Trust is a language she has forgotten.
- **Calculative, not spontaneous** — every move weighed, every risk assessed. She
  does not gamble; she hunts.

## Delairah — the Aurelis
*Portrait: `Delairah.png` · Player: Isa*

From the verdant world of **Pandora**, where forests glow with living light and rivers
sing with alien resonance. Taken from their family in childhood by interstellar
slavers and treated as little more than a power source — a rare energy-being bound to
machines they did not understand.

In adolescence they learned in secret to channel their own inner pulse, turning the
power their captors exploited against them. They escaped an uprising in a stolen pod,
adrift until crash-landing on **Taldori**, a frontier world where survival was
measured in ingenuity.

There a **sorcery clan** taught them discipline and focus — meditation, breath and chi
rituals — to harness the warp: folding space, bending gravity, dissolving the boundary
between matter and thought. Those mentors also instilled reverence for the natural
world: every root, leaf and grain of soil reflects cosmic truth. Their years on
Taldori gave them martial mastery and a philosopher's spirit, attuned to agriculture
and growth — planting as faith, harvest as renewal, soil as a mirror of the stars.

Now they wander with a dual hunger: to explore the ends of the universe, and to
uncover **the seed of existence** — an artifact hidden in this star region, connected
to the very origin of life. The corporate dominion ruling these systems may already
hold clues, perhaps even pieces, of it.

## Zuk the Truck — the Drakari Juggernaut
*Portrait: `Zuk.png` · Species: Drakari*

Once a simple pet lizard owned by a janitor who scrubbed toilets on a backwater base.
His life was small but content — heat lamps, crickets, the world passing by behind
glass. Then he slipped his tank and scuttled into the experimental **Trapezatron
9000**, a machine built to warp biology into sentient humanoid forms. Out came Zuk
the Truck: a towering Drakari Juggernaut of scales, claws and rage.

Finding piles of his own molted skins, Zuk mistook them for the corpses of his fallen
comrades. Believing his masters had slain them, he rampaged across the facility,
smashing everything — including the janitor who had loved him.

He now works as a mercenary-for-hire, paid not in money but in **Omicron Sepher
Gnats**, rare and juicy space-flies. He fights wherever the gnats lead, convinced
destiny is hidden in the buzzing of wings.

> Catchphrase: *"That's one way to make a buk."*

## Jedediah "Jed" Rourke — the Engineer
*Portrait: `Jed.png` · Class: Cyborgian Engineer*

At **92**, Jed is a testament to both age and technology. A lean 5'7", his frame is a
patchwork of organic and synthetic. His face is a roadmap of wrinkles telling of a
hard life under a desert sun; what is left of his hair is a thin ring of stark white
around a bald, weathered scalp. He wears simple horn-rimmed glasses — a peculiar
choice for a man with a gleaming cybernetic left eye.

A weary old traveller and master mechanic who once ran a humble repair shop on a
remote arid planet, now on a final journey. His southern drawl and quiet wisdom
suggest a man who has seen more than his share of the galaxy. An old vet with a
unique affinity for anything mechanical, able to fix complex systems by touch.

He does not speak about his past — only that he knows he does not have much time left,
so he is focused on the here and now. He wanders the spaceways helping who he can,
hoping to leave this existence **without regrets**.

## Sokka of the Veyr — the Harmonist
*Portrait: `Sokka.png` · Species: Virellian*

Born beneath the storm-skin of an ocean world whose maps were written in sound.

Among the **Virellian**, reefs are not geography but memory: living coral-lattices hum
with layered harmonics that guide migration, mark safe trenches, and carry the
**reef-song** used in rites of naming and healing. As a hatchling Sokka learned to
navigate by resonance the way others learn to read stars. His skin patterns flared
bioluminescent in answer to the reef's pulse, and elders said his tone was unusually
clean and steady — *anchoring*. The kind of voice that holds others together.

Then **AstraCalyx Meridian** arrived. Not with soldiers, but with contracts, orbital
permits and drilling rigs that sang a dead frequency through the water. They mined the
reefs for exotic crystalline matrices — perfect for starship drive regulators — and the
reef-song began to fracture. Whole sections fell silent. Newborn Virellians hatched
**off-key**: seizures, fluid collapse, minds that could not hold shape. The elders
called it **Dissonance Birth**. The corporation called it an acceptable loss.

Sokka joined a resistance cell moving like currents through occupied water — smugglers,
saboteurs, reef-guardians who stole medicine, rescued hatchlings and sabotaged
extraction rigs. In raids he carried no blade. He carried **resonance**: tuning wounded
bodies back into coherence after decompression burns and shrapnel tears, forcing broken
lungs to remember breath and panicked hearts back into tempo. He became a myth to his
people — a Harmonist who could stitch life with sound.

# Lore reconciliation

Where the player backstories meet the console's obelisk lore. Nothing below has been
changed in the console — these are decisions for the GM.

### 1. Othuun and the obelisk entity — the big one

The console's transmissions already run a shard thread that rhymes with Vorvane's
backstory almost word for word:

> THE LUMINARIS CALL THEIR SHARD-LINK STABLE.
> EVERY SHARD THEY HOLD IS A MOUTH THEY ARE KEEPING SHUT.

"A mouth they are keeping shut" and *Maw of dead suns* are the same image. Three ways
to resolve it:

- **Unify.** The obelisk entity *is* Othuun, or the largest surviving shard of it. The
  Forerunners built the obelisk as containment — "THE FORERUNNERS DIED HOLDING IT
  CLOSED. YOU ARE PRYING." The 1,000 shards are the fragmentation; Luminaris shard-tech
  and Shoyunuki's resonance sweep are both unwitting handling of Othuun. This is the
  tightest fit and costs nothing to adopt.
- **Sibling.** Two separate void intelligences from the same era — the war in heaven woke
  more than one thing. Keeps Vorvane's arc personal and separate.
- **Rival.** The obelisk entity is what *killed* Othuun, or what the weapon was built
  from. Turns the transmissions adversarial to Vorvane specifically.

If you pick **unify**, the level-2 line "IT HAS BEEN WAITING FOR SOMETHING TO NOTICE
BACK" becomes 49,000 years of waiting, which lands harder.

### 2. The unnamed mega-corp

Vorvane's backstory has an explicit blank: *"(insert giga mega corp name here)"*.
**Shoyunuki Corp** already owns the console the players use, ran the resonance sweep
that "has read DEGRADED for eleven years", and is named in the level-2 transmission.
Dropping Shoyunuki into that blank means the players' own terminal is issued by the
company that farmed 295 Othuun shards into symbiotes.

Note this creates **three** corporate actors — Shoyunuki, AstraCalyx Meridian (Sokka),
and Nyra's unnamed "faceless order". Worth deciding whether those are three companies,
or one company and two subsidiaries.

### 3. Nyra and Sokka share a wound

Both are ocean worlds killed by corporate extraction; both lost their people to it.
Nyra's is poisoning by waste tankers, Sokka's is reef-mining for crystalline matrices.
Nyra is *hunting the name on the order*. Sokka already **knows** his: AstraCalyx
Meridian. If they are the same event or the same parent company, Sokka is carrying the
answer to Nyra's entire arc and does not know it — a strong table moment that costs
nothing to set up.

### 4. Two Starseers, two orders

Caolan serves the **Rugi**; Quill was raised by the **Starseer Guild**. Neither term
appears in the design docs. Are they the same institution under two names, two
branches, or rivals? Quill left because the Guild came to fear him; Caolan is
straining the Rugi's limits on "violent usage of power" right now. If they are the same
order, Quill is Caolan's future and Caolan is Quill's past — they should meet.

### 5. Delairah's "seed of existence"

An artifact connected to the origin of life, hidden in this star region, with corporate
interests possibly already holding **pieces**. Structurally identical to the shard
premise. Either it is the same object viewed through a hopeful lens rather than a
hungry one, or the region contains two ancient distributed artifacts — which is a lot.
Recommend deciding before the players compare notes.

### 6. Names not yet in the design docs

Present in backstories, absent from `SPECIES-DESIGN.md` / `CLASS-DESIGN.md`:
**Rugi**, **Aurelis**, **Veyr**, **Othuun**, **Pandora**, **Taldori**,
**AstraCalyx Meridian**, **Dr. Junias Xerthlot**, **Trapezatron 9000**,
**Omicron Sepher Gnats**, **Dissonance Birth**.

Already present: Starseer (class), Virellian and Drakari (species), Cyborgian Engineer
(Jed's class), Shoyunuki, Luminaris, Nihyros, Forerunners.
