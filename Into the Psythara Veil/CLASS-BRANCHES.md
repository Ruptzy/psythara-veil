# Class Branches — all 33, production run

*Authored under SKILL-TREE-RULEBOOK.md law, from the Specter mold proven in
VERTICAL-SLICE.md: two nodes upgrade signature A, two touch signature B, a
stance and a conversion tie the kit together, and a capstone **patches the
class ability's actual payload** — never prose.*

Every branch: 7 nodes · 2 pts each, capstone 5 (T5, ≥8 pts in branch) · node 6
is T4 · **2 RANK nodes of 7 = 29%, under the 40% law, every branch** · every
branch carries a stance, a conversion, and at least one trigger.

Shorthand: "r2: +1 use" = second rank adds one use per cryo cycle. "while X"
conditions are all from the sheet-known whitelist or are [TAP] states. DCs are
always computed and printed by the sheet.

Specter's branch lives in VERTICAL-SLICE.md — this file is the other 32.
