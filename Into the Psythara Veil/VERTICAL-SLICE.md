# Vertical Slice — "Vex", Raxen Specter, levels 1–20

*One complete character walked through every level, spending real points under
the rulebook's law. This is the proof-of-agony document: if the choices here
feel obvious or painless, the pricing is wrong and we fix it before 33 classes
inherit it.*

Status: **draft for DM sign-off.** The Specter branch and Raxen vein written
here are the production prototypes for steps 4 and 5.

---

## The character

**Vex** — a Raxen scavenger who found a Specter's scout rifle in a derelict and
taught herself the rest. Ranged style → Lattice trunk → **Deadeye** fork.
Ghostwire affinity (Specter class key). The fantasy: *the shot comes from
nowhere, and the cameras saw nothing.*

Existing kit this build touches:
- **Recon Sweep** (3/cycle) — reveal hidden 60 ft, party +20% accuracy, 3 rounds
- **Ghost Protocol** (2/cycle) — invisible until first attack, +6 AC, +20 ft
- **Ghoststep** (species, 2/cycle) — cloak 2 rounds, +15 ft, first attack advantage

## The Specter class branch (production prototype)

7 nodes · 2 pts each, capstone 5 · mix: 2 rank of 7 = 29% ✓ · total 17 pts

- **SP1 · Wider Net** · RANK ×2 · [AUTO+TABLE] — Recon Sweep reaches 90 ft
  [TABLE]; r2: +1 Recon Sweep use per cycle [AUTO].
- **SP2 · Marked for Death** · TRIGGER · [TAP] — tap when you hit an enemy
  Recon Sweep revealed this encounter: that hit gains +2d6.
- **SP3 · Silent Running** · STANCE · [AUTO+TABLE] — toggle: kills made under
  Ghost Protocol don't break the invisibility [TABLE]; −15% damage while
  active [AUTO]. Assassination vs. output, chosen fight by fight.
- **SP4 · Ghost Overdraw** · CONVERSION · [AUTO] — at will: burn one Recon
  Sweep charge to extend a running Ghost Protocol by 2 rounds.
- **SP5 · Phantom Decoy** · ACTIVE 2/cycle · [TAP+TABLE] — project a false you.
  The first attack that would hit you each encounter strikes the decoy instead
  (tap to consume it).
- **SP6 · Kill From Nowhere** (T4) · RANK ×2 · [AUTO] — +10% / +20% ranged
  damage **while Ghost Protocol is active** (conditional, so it's allowed to be
  big).
- **SP7 · CAPSTONE — ERASURE** (T5) · 5 · [AUTO+TABLE] — Ghost Protocol
  transformed: attacking no longer ends the invisibility — each shot instead
  bleeds 2 points off its AC bonus (fading, not breaking) [AUTO], and your
  first attack under each activation is an automatic critical [AUTO].

*The formula on display: two nodes upgrade signature #1, two upgrade signature
#2, one stance and one conversion tie the two signatures together, and the
capstone rewrites the class's defining ability. This shape repeats 33 times.*

## One round of play at level 18 (the texture test)

Ghost Protocol up, **Silent Running toggled on** (−15% damage, kills stay
silent). ERASURE makes her opening shot an auto-crit — she takes it, the guard
drops, the cloak *fades 2 AC instead of breaking*. Recon Sweep has two charges;
she burns one through **Ghost Overdraw** to stretch the cloak two more rounds
rather than revealing the room. Next turn a survivor wings her — **tap: Phase
Fur**, damage halved. His buddy's shot would land too — **tap: Phantom Decoy**
eats it. She never rolled a defense. She made five decisions.

That's the taxonomy doing its job: one round, five choices, zero of them "add
my bonus."

## System Damage, felt

Vex goes down at level 19. Her highest tier is T5: she must choose between
ERASURE and The Long Silence going dark. She kills the trunk capstone — the
auto-crit *is* her — and until cryo (or a V₡ Field Repair) her obelisk shows
one dead socket where the declared shot used to live. The penalty is a build
decision, which is exactly what it was designed to be.

## What the slice proves — and what to watch

1. **Banking is structural, not incidental.** Capstones force 1–2 quiet levels
   of saving. Verdict: feature — anticipation is content. But if you want no
   dead levels, capstones could drop to 4 pts. *(Recommend: keep 5.)*
2. **T5 is a two-purchase window.** Levels 17–20 yield 12 points; two capstones
   eat 10. A build gets both only by arriving with a plan. Correct tension.
3. **The class branch completes around L14 if prioritized** — identity peaks
   mid-game, trunk power finishes the run. Feels right; flag if you want
   capstones to land earlier.
4. **Discipline dips cost real combat power** (level 8 and 12 bought zero
   damage). The player who hacks *chose* to hack. As designed.
5. **The 40% ratio law held without strain** in both authored branches — no
   node had to be contorted to satisfy it.

**Sign-off checklist:** the branch formula (2+2 signature upgrades, stance,
conversion, capstone transform) · the vein formula (buff / passive+rider /
defensive tap) · capstone price at 5 · banking as intended behavior. Approve
these and the 33-class production run starts from a proven mold.
