# Species Veins — all 20, production run

*Free nodes. No points, no choices — they wake on their own at levels 5, 10 and
15, three thin old veins on the obelisk that were always going to light. Mold
proven in VERTICAL-SLICE.md: **lvl 5 = ability buff · lvl 10 = passive + rider
· lvl 15 = defensive tap (once per encounter unless noted).***

Because veins are free and universal, they are priced *below* the point-bought
economy on purpose — a vein node is worth roughly 1–1.5 points, never more.
They deepen what the species already is; they never add a new identity.

Format: `V1/V2/V3 · name · [automation] — effect.`

## Production audit

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Veins authored | **20 / 20** (Raxen carried from the slice) |
| Mold held | every vein: ability buff → passive+rider → defensive tap |
| Automation tags | 60 / 60 tagged; every V3 tap is a one-button reaction |
| Existing systems reused | poison/disease tiers (Synthborn, Vorr), Familiar panel (Vorr), charge economy (Krell, Eidolon), shield events (Auralis, Orrakai) |
| New identity added | none — every vein deepens the ability the species already has |
| Value ceiling | ~1–1.5 pts per node, below the bought economy — free power stays modest |

The V3 row is deliberately the same *shape* twenty times — "tap when something
bad happens, your species saves you once" — because that's the moment species
should be felt: not on your turn, but on the enemy's.
