# Which screenshots to take

Take these from **your own console** (the full one on your Desktop) — it has the
artwork and looks considerably better than the stripped build. Nothing in these
four views shows a third-party item name, so they are safe to post.

Windows: `Win + Shift + S` → drag a region → it copies to your clipboard, and
saves to `Pictures/Screenshots` if you press `Win + PrtScn` instead.

---

### 1. The alert gate  ← lead with this one
Take damage worth more than half your shields + health in one go (drop shields to
0, then set current HP low). The red **HEAVY DAMAGE TAKEN** card takes the screen.

*Why it leads:* it's the clearest single image of a deliberate UX decision —
interrupting only when it's warranted, and requiring an acknowledgement.

### 2. Vitals
Scroll to the Vitals panel. Press **"how is this worked out?"** so the formula
box is open in the shot.

*Why:* shows progressive disclosure, the grouped number/action rows, and the
animated vitals sign in the header.

### 3. Active Effects with something armed
Use Focus Fire (or any stim), then screenshot the Active Effects row.

*Why:* every chip in that row is generated from one modifier pipeline. It's the
engine made visible.

### 4. Glossary terms in context
Any panel with several underlined terms — the Origin panel or an ability card.
Hover one so the tooltip is showing.

*Why:* the colour-coded teaching system, which is the most transferable idea here.

### Optional 5: phone width
Narrow your browser window to roughly 390px wide and screenshot the top of the
sheet. Shows it reflows properly.

---

**Order matters on LinkedIn** — the first image is what stops the scroll. Lead
with the alert gate or Vitals, not a wall of text.
